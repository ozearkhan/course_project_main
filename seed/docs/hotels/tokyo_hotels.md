# General Hotel Policies

- Government-issued identification or passport is required during check-in.
- Early check-in and late check-out are subject to room availability.
- Smoking is prohibited in most guest rooms.
- Complimentary Wi-Fi is available in nearly all hotels.
- Breakfast inclusion depends on the selected room package.
- Airport transfer services may require advance reservation.
- Additional accommodation taxes may apply depending on room rates.

---

# Booking Recommendations

- Book hotels near major railway stations such as Tokyo Station or Shinjuku Station for easier transportation.
- Luxury hotels in Ginza, Roppongi, and Marunouchi generally have higher room rates.
- Families should consider hotels offering larger rooms and child-friendly amenities.
- Travelers visiting during Cherry Blossom season (March–April) should book accommodations several months in advance.
- Purchase a Suica or PASMO card for convenient travel from your hotel to major attractions.
