# General Hotel Policies

- Valid government-issued identification is required during check-in.
- Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability.
- Cancellation policies vary by booking type.
- Most hotels offer complimentary Wi-Fi.
- Breakfast may or may not be included depending on the room plan.
- Pets are allowed only in selected hotels.
- Additional city taxes may be collected during check-in.

---

# Booking Recommendations

- Reserve rooms at least 30 days in advance during summer and holiday seasons.
- Choose accommodations near Metro stations for convenient transportation.
- Luxury hotels around the Eiffel Tower and Champs-Élysées generally have higher room rates.
- Families should consider hotels offering family rooms and complimentary breakfast.
- Business travelers may prefer hotels with conference facilities and airport transportation.
