# General Hotel Policies

- A valid passport or government-issued identification is required during check-in.
- Early check-in and late check-out are subject to room availability.
- Complimentary Wi-Fi is available in all hotels.
- Breakfast inclusion depends on the selected room package.
- Some luxury hotels require a refundable security deposit.
- Airport transfer services should be booked in advance.
- Tourism fees may be collected during check-in according to Dubai regulations.

---

# Booking Recommendations

- Book accommodations at least one month in advance during the winter travel season (November to March).
- Downtown Dubai is recommended for sightseeing and shopping.
- Palm Jumeirah is ideal for luxury beach vacations.
- Dubai Marina and JBR are excellent choices for nightlife and waterfront dining.
- Business travelers should consider hotels in Business Bay or DIFC for easy access to commercial districts.
- Families may prefer resorts that offer kids' clubs, private beaches, and swimming pools.
