# General Hotel Policies

- Valid passport or government-issued identification is required during check-in.
- Early check-in and late check-out depend on room availability.
- Complimentary Wi-Fi is available in most hotels.
- Breakfast inclusion depends on the selected room package.
- Airport transfers should be booked in advance where available.
- Extra beds and cribs are subject to hotel policies.
- Some resorts may require a refundable security deposit.

---

# Booking Recommendations

- Reserve accommodations at least one month before travel during peak season (June to August and December).
- Nusa Dua is ideal for luxury beach vacations.
- Ubud is recommended for nature, wellness, and cultural experiences.
- Seminyak is popular for shopping, nightlife, and restaurants.
- Jimbaran offers excellent seafood restaurants and quieter beaches.
- Travelers planning island tours should confirm hotel pickup availability before booking excursions.
