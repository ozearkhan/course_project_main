# General Hotel Policies

- A valid passport or government-issued identification is required during check-in.
- Early check-in and late check-out are subject to room availability.
- Complimentary Wi-Fi is available throughout most hotels.
- Breakfast may or may not be included depending on the selected room plan.
- Security deposits may be required during check-in.
- Airport transportation should be reserved in advance if required.
- Extra beds are available only for selected room categories.

---

# Booking Recommendations

- Reserve accommodations at least 30 to 45 days before travel during school holidays and major events.
- Marina Bay is ideal for luxury stays and iconic city views.
- Orchard Road is recommended for shopping enthusiasts.
- Chinatown and Clarke Quay are excellent choices for nightlife and local cuisine.
- Families should prioritize hotels offering family rooms and swimming pools.
- Business travelers may prefer hotels located near Marina Bay Financial Centre and Suntec Convention Centre.
