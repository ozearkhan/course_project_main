# Refund Policy

## Overview

TripPilot is committed to providing a transparent and efficient refund process for eligible cancellations. Refunds are processed according to the cancellation policy of the booked service, supplier terms, and applicable payment regulations.

The refund amount depends on the type of booking, cancellation timing, supplier charges, and applicable service fees.

---

## Refund Eligibility

Customers may be eligible for a refund in the following situations:

- Eligible hotel booking cancellation
- Eligible flight cancellation
- Holiday package cancellation
- Tour or activity cancellation
- Duplicate payment
- Failed booking with successful payment
- Supplier cancellation
- Travel service unavailable due to operational issues

Refund eligibility is determined after verifying the booking and applicable supplier policies.

---

## Non-Refundable Items

The following charges are generally non-refundable:

- Visa Processing Fees
- Travel Insurance Premiums
- Payment Gateway Charges
- Currency Conversion Charges
- Promotional Booking Fees
- Processing Charges
- Certain Airline Fare Types
- Last-Minute Promotional Offers

Supplier-specific terms may impose additional restrictions.

---

## Refund Processing Time

Estimated refund timelines are:

| Payment Method | Estimated Processing Time |
|----------------|---------------------------|
| Credit Card | 5–10 Business Days |
| Debit Card | 5–10 Business Days |
| UPI | 2–5 Business Days |
| Net Banking | 3–7 Business Days |
| Digital Wallet | 2–5 Business Days |

Processing times may vary depending on the customer's bank or payment provider.

---

## Refund Method

Refunds are issued using the original payment method whenever possible.

Examples include:

- Credit Card
- Debit Card
- UPI
- Net Banking
- Digital Wallet

Cash refunds are generally not provided.

---

## Partial Refunds

Certain bookings may qualify for a partial refund.

Examples include:

- Hotel cancellations after the free cancellation period.
- Holiday package cancellations with supplier charges.
- Airline ticket cancellations with applicable penalties.
- Tour bookings with operator cancellation fees.

The final refund amount will be calculated after deducting applicable charges.

---

## Refund Approval Process

After receiving a refund request, TripPilot will:

1. Verify the booking details.
2. Review the supplier's cancellation policy.
3. Calculate applicable deductions.
4. Obtain supplier confirmation if required.
5. Initiate the refund to the original payment method.

Customers will receive email notifications regarding the status of their refund request.

---

## Supplier-Initiated Refunds

If a supplier cancels a booking due to operational reasons, customers may receive:

- Full Refund
- Partial Refund
- Alternative Booking
- Travel Credit or Voucher

The available options depend on the supplier's policy.

---

## Duplicate Payments

If a customer accidentally makes multiple payments for the same booking:

- The duplicate transaction should be reported immediately.
- Payment records will be verified.
- Eligible duplicate payments will be refunded after verification.

---

## Failed Booking with Successful Payment

Occasionally, payment may be successful while the booking is not confirmed.

In such cases:

- The booking will be reviewed.
- If confirmation cannot be obtained, the full payment will be refunded.
- Customers will be informed through their registered email address.

---

## Refund Status

Customers can check the status of their refund through:

- TripPilot Customer Portal
- Mobile Application
- Customer Support
- Registered Email Notifications

The following information may be required:

- Booking Reference Number
- Registered Email Address
- Contact Number

---

## Chargebacks

Customers are encouraged to contact TripPilot Customer Support before initiating a chargeback through their bank.

Unauthorized or duplicate chargebacks may delay future refund requests while investigations are completed.

---

## Customer Responsibilities

Customers should:

- Submit cancellation requests promptly.
- Verify refund eligibility before requesting a refund.
- Retain payment receipts and booking confirmations.
- Ensure bank account or payment details remain active until the refund is completed.
- Contact customer support if the refund is not received within the expected timeframe.

---

## Important Notes

- Refund processing begins only after cancellation has been successfully confirmed.
- Processing times exclude weekends and public holidays.
- Banks and payment providers may require additional time to credit refunded amounts.
- Supplier-specific refund policies take precedence over general TripPilot refund guidelines.
- TripPilot will provide a refund reference number once the refund has been initiated.
