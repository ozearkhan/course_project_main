# Payment Policy

## Overview

TripPilot offers multiple secure payment methods to ensure a smooth booking experience for customers worldwide. All payments are processed through trusted payment gateways that use industry-standard encryption and security protocols.

Customers are responsible for ensuring sufficient funds are available before completing a booking.

---

## Accepted Payment Methods

TripPilot currently accepts the following payment methods:

### Credit Cards

Supported card networks include:

- Visa
- Mastercard
- American Express
- Discover (where supported)

---

### Debit Cards

Most domestic and international debit cards issued by recognized financial institutions are accepted.

---

### Unified Payments Interface (UPI)

Customers in supported regions can pay using:

- Google Pay
- PhonePe
- Paytm
- BHIM UPI
- Other UPI-enabled applications

---

### Net Banking

Payments can be made through supported banking partners using secure online banking portals.

---

### Digital Wallets

Depending on the customer's location, supported wallets may include:

- Apple Pay
- Google Pay
- PayPal
- Samsung Wallet

Availability varies by country.

---

## Payment Currency

TripPilot supports payments in multiple currencies.

Commonly supported currencies include:

- USD – United States Dollar
- EUR – Euro
- GBP – British Pound Sterling
- SGD – Singapore Dollar
- AED – UAE Dirham
- JPY – Japanese Yen
- IDR – Indonesian Rupiah
- INR – Indian Rupee

Currency availability depends on the selected destination and payment gateway.

---

## Currency Conversion

If the payment currency differs from the card's billing currency:

- Currency conversion is performed by the customer's bank or payment provider.
- Exchange rates are determined by the issuing bank.
- Additional foreign transaction charges may apply.

TripPilot is not responsible for exchange rate fluctuations or bank conversion fees.

---

## Payment Security

TripPilot prioritizes payment security.

Security measures include:

- SSL Encryption
- PCI DSS Compliant Payment Processing
- Secure Payment Gateways
- Fraud Detection Systems
- Tokenized Payment Processing

TripPilot does not store complete credit or debit card information on its servers.

---

## Payment Confirmation

After successful payment, customers receive:

- Payment Confirmation Email
- Booking Confirmation
- Booking Reference Number
- Digital Receipt
- Travel Itinerary (if applicable)

Customers should retain these documents for future reference.

---

## Payment Failure

A payment may fail due to:

- Insufficient Funds
- Incorrect Card Details
- Expired Card
- Bank Authorization Failure
- Network Connectivity Issues
- Payment Gateway Timeout

If payment fails, customers may retry using the same or an alternative payment method.

---

## Partial Payments

Certain holiday packages and group bookings may allow partial payments.

Typical payment schedule:

- Initial Deposit during booking
- Remaining Balance before departure

Failure to complete the remaining payment before the due date may result in automatic booking cancellation.

---

## Refunds

Eligible refunds are processed according to the TripPilot Refund Policy.

Approved refunds are credited to the original payment method used during booking.

Estimated processing times depend on:

- Payment Method
- Bank Processing Time
- Payment Provider

---

## Duplicate Payments

If multiple payments are made accidentally for the same booking:

1. Contact TripPilot Customer Support immediately.
2. Provide the Booking Reference Number.
3. Share payment confirmation details.

After verification, eligible duplicate payments will be refunded.

---

## Failed Booking After Successful Payment

In rare situations, payment may be successful but booking confirmation may fail.

If this occurs:

- The booking will be reviewed.
- Confirmation will be attempted with the supplier.
- If confirmation is unsuccessful, the payment will be refunded according to the Refund Policy.

Customers will receive updates via their registered email address.

---

## Fraud Prevention

To protect customers, TripPilot may perform additional verification for high-value or suspicious transactions.

Verification may include:

- Identity Verification
- Card Ownership Verification
- OTP Authentication
- Additional Banking Confirmation

Bookings may remain pending until verification is completed.

---

## Customer Responsibilities

Customers should:

- Verify payment details before confirming payment.
- Use payment methods registered in their own name whenever possible.
- Keep payment receipts until travel is completed.
- Ensure sufficient account balance before making payments.
- Contact customer support immediately if unauthorized transactions are detected.

---

## Important Notes

- Booking confirmation is generated only after successful payment authorization.
- Prices displayed during booking are subject to availability until payment is completed.
- Banks may impose additional service charges or foreign transaction fees.
- TripPilot reserves the right to cancel unpaid reservations after the payment deadline expires.
- Customers should never share OTPs, PINs, passwords, or payment credentials with anyone claiming to represent TripPilot.
