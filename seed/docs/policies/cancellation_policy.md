# Cancellation Policy

## Overview

TripPilot understands that travel plans may change due to personal, professional, or unforeseen circumstances. This cancellation policy explains the terms and conditions applicable when cancelling hotel bookings, flights, holiday packages, sightseeing tours, airport transfers, and other travel services booked through TripPilot.

Cancellation charges vary depending on the supplier, destination, travel dates, and the time remaining before departure.

---

## Hotel Booking Cancellation

Hotel cancellation charges are generally based on the time at which the cancellation request is received.

| Time Before Check-in | Refund Eligibility |
|----------------------|-------------------|
| More than 72 hours | 100% Refund (excluding service fees) |
| 48–72 hours | 75% Refund |
| 24–48 hours | 50% Refund |
| Less than 24 hours | No Refund |
| No Show | No Refund |

Certain hotels may have stricter cancellation policies during holidays, festivals, and peak travel seasons.

---

## Flight Cancellation

Flight cancellations are governed by the airline's fare rules.

Cancellation charges may depend on:

- Fare Type
- Airline Policy
- Route
- Time Before Departure
- Promotional Fare Conditions

Non-refundable airline tickets generally cannot be refunded after confirmation.

Any applicable airline cancellation fee will be deducted before processing refunds.

---

## Holiday Package Cancellation

Holiday package cancellations are processed according to the following schedule.

| Cancellation Before Departure | Cancellation Charge |
|-------------------------------|---------------------|
| More than 30 days | 10% of Package Cost |
| 15–30 days | 25% of Package Cost |
| 7–14 days | 50% of Package Cost |
| Less than 7 days | 100% of Package Cost |

Some third-party services included within the package may have separate cancellation policies.

---

## Tour and Activity Cancellation

Cancellation rules for tours and sightseeing activities depend on the tour operator.

Typical policy:

- More than 48 hours: Full Refund
- 24–48 hours: 50% Refund
- Less than 24 hours: No Refund

Adventure activities, event tickets, and attraction passes may be completely non-refundable.

---

## Airport Transfer Cancellation

Airport transfer bookings may generally be cancelled:

- More than 24 hours before pickup:
  Full Refund

- Less than 24 hours:
  Cancellation charges may apply.

No refunds are available for missed pickups caused by delayed customer arrival.

---

## How to Cancel a Booking

Customers may request cancellation through:

- TripPilot Customer Support
- Registered Email Address
- Online Booking Portal
- Mobile Application

Cancellation requests should include:

- Booking Reference Number
- Customer Name
- Contact Information
- Reason for Cancellation

---

## Cancellation Confirmation

A cancellation request is considered complete only after the customer receives:

- Cancellation Confirmation Email
- Cancellation Reference Number

Customers should retain these documents until the refund process has been completed.

---

## Non-Refundable Bookings

The following bookings are generally non-refundable:

- Promotional Offers
- Flash Sale Bookings
- Last-Minute Deals
- Certain Airline Tickets
- Special Event Packages
- Visa Processing Fees
- Travel Insurance Premiums

Applicable supplier terms always take precedence.

---

## Force Majeure

TripPilot is not responsible for cancellations resulting from events beyond reasonable control, including:

- Natural Disasters
- Floods
- Earthquakes
- Hurricanes
- Political Unrest
- War
- Government Travel Restrictions
- Pandemics
- Airport Closures
- Airline Operational Disruptions

Refund eligibility in such situations depends on supplier policies.

---

## Customer Responsibilities

Customers should:

- Review cancellation terms before confirming bookings.
- Notify TripPilot immediately if travel plans change.
- Verify airline and hotel cancellation deadlines.
- Keep copies of cancellation confirmations.
- Contact customer support if a cancellation confirmation is not received.

---

## Important Notes

- Cancellation charges are calculated based on the date and time the cancellation request is received.
- Processing a cancellation does not automatically mean a refund has been approved.
- Supplier-specific cancellation policies override general TripPilot policies where applicable.
- Any applicable bank charges, payment gateway fees, or currency conversion fees may be deducted from the final refund amount.
- Customers are encouraged to purchase comprehensive travel insurance to protect against cancellation-related financial losses.
