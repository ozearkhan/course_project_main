# Baggage Policy

## Overview

TripPilot provides travel booking services in partnership with multiple airlines. Baggage allowances are determined by the operating airline, ticket type, travel class, and destination.

Customers are responsible for reviewing baggage limits before departure. Excess baggage charges are determined by the airline and must be paid directly to the airline where applicable.

---

## Cabin (Carry-on) Baggage

Cabin baggage may be carried into the aircraft cabin and must comply with airline size and weight restrictions.

Typical cabin baggage allowance:

| Travel Class | Weight Allowance |
|--------------|-----------------|
| Economy Class | Up to 7 kg |
| Premium Economy | Up to 10 kg |
| Business Class | Up to 14 kg |
| First Class | Up to 15 kg |

Typical cabin baggage dimensions:

- Length: 55 cm
- Width: 35 cm
- Height: 25 cm

These limits may vary by airline.

---

## Checked Baggage

Checked baggage is transported in the aircraft cargo hold.

Typical baggage allowance:

| Travel Class | Weight Allowance |
|--------------|-----------------|
| Economy Class | 20–25 kg |
| Premium Economy | 25–30 kg |
| Business Class | 30–40 kg |
| First Class | 40–50 kg |

Certain airlines use a piece-based baggage system instead of a weight-based system.

Customers should verify baggage allowances before departure.

---

## Personal Item

Most airlines permit one personal item in addition to cabin baggage.

Examples include:

- Laptop Bag
- Handbag
- Small Backpack
- Camera Bag
- Diaper Bag

The personal item should fit underneath the seat in front of the passenger.

---

## Excess Baggage

Additional charges apply when baggage exceeds the permitted allowance.

Excess baggage fees depend on:

- Airline
- Destination
- Weight
- Number of Bags
- Route

Purchasing additional baggage online before departure is generally more economical than paying at the airport.

---

## Oversized Baggage

Oversized baggage includes luggage exceeding the airline's permitted dimensions.

Examples include:

- Golf Equipment
- Surfboards
- Musical Instruments
- Sports Equipment
- Large Suitcases

Advance notification may be required.

Additional handling charges may apply.

---

## Special Baggage

Special baggage includes:

- Wheelchairs
- Baby Strollers
- Bicycle Cases
- Ski Equipment
- Snowboards
- Diving Equipment
- Medical Equipment

Transportation depends on airline policies and aircraft capacity.

Customers should notify the airline in advance whenever possible.

---

## Restricted Items in Cabin Baggage

The following items are generally prohibited inside cabin baggage:

- Firearms
- Ammunition
- Explosives
- Fireworks
- Flammable Liquids
- Toxic Chemicals
- Sharp Weapons
- Large Knives
- Self-defense Weapons

Security authorities may confiscate prohibited items.

---

## Restricted Items in Checked Baggage

The following items are generally prohibited in checked baggage:

- Explosives
- Gas Cylinders
- Highly Flammable Materials
- Corrosive Chemicals
- Radioactive Materials
- Dangerous Goods
- Illegal Substances

Airlines may impose additional restrictions.

---

## Liquids in Cabin Baggage

For international travel, liquids should generally comply with the following guidelines:

- Maximum container size: 100 ml
- Containers should fit inside one transparent, resealable plastic bag.
- Total bag capacity should not exceed one liter.

Exceptions may apply for:

- Baby Food
- Prescription Medicines
- Medical Liquids

Additional screening may be required.

---

## Fragile Items

Passengers should carry valuable or fragile items in their cabin baggage whenever possible.

Examples include:

- Laptops
- Cameras
- Mobile Phones
- Jewelry
- Important Documents
- Prescription Medicines

TripPilot and airlines are not responsible for damage resulting from improper packing.

---

## Lost or Delayed Baggage

If baggage is lost or delayed:

1. Report the issue immediately at the airline baggage service counter.
2. Obtain a Property Irregularity Report (PIR).
3. Retain baggage tags and boarding passes.
4. Contact the airline for baggage tracking updates.
5. Notify your travel insurance provider if applicable.

Compensation is subject to airline policies and international aviation regulations.

---

## Customer Responsibilities

Customers should:

- Verify baggage allowance before departure.
- Label all checked baggage with contact information.
- Arrive at the airport early for baggage check-in.
- Avoid carrying prohibited items.
- Purchase additional baggage allowance before travel if necessary.
- Keep valuables and important documents in cabin baggage.

---

## Important Notes

- Baggage policies differ between airlines.
- Codeshare flights may have different baggage rules than the operating carrier.
- Excess baggage fees are collected by the airline.
- Security authorities have the right to inspect all baggage.
- Customers should consult their airline for the latest baggage regulations before travel.
