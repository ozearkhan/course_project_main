# Booking Policy

## Overview

TripPilot provides hotel bookings, travel packages, sightseeing tours, airport transfers, and other travel-related services. This booking policy outlines the rules, responsibilities, and conditions applicable when making reservations through TripPilot.

By confirming a booking, customers agree to the terms and conditions described in this policy.

---

## Booking Confirmation

A booking is considered confirmed only after:

- Successful payment has been received.
- A booking confirmation email has been sent.
- A unique booking reference number has been generated.
- The supplier (hotel, airline, or tour operator) has confirmed availability.

Customers should review their booking confirmation immediately and report any discrepancies within 24 hours.

---

## Information Required During Booking

Customers may be required to provide:

- Full Name (as per Passport)
- Date of Birth
- Nationality
- Passport Number (for international travel)
- Contact Number
- Email Address
- Emergency Contact Details
- Special Requests (if any)

Providing incorrect information may result in booking delays or cancellation.

---

## Hotel Bookings

Hotel reservations are subject to:

- Room availability
- Seasonal pricing
- Hotel policies
- Occupancy limits
- Local tourism taxes

Room preferences such as:

- Twin Beds
- King Bed
- High Floor
- Non-Smoking Room
- Connecting Rooms

are treated as requests and cannot always be guaranteed.

---

## Flight Bookings

Flight bookings are subject to:

- Airline availability
- Fare rules
- Government regulations
- Airport taxes
- Airline baggage policies

Flight schedules may change without prior notice. Customers should verify flight timings before departure.

---

## Tour and Activity Bookings

Tours and activities require:

- Advance reservation
- Confirmation from the tour operator
- Timely arrival at the meeting point

Some activities have age, health, or safety restrictions.

TripPilot is not responsible for missed tours caused by late arrival.

---

## Booking Modifications

Customers may request changes to:

- Travel Dates
- Passenger Information
- Hotel Category
- Tour Selection

Modification requests:

- Are subject to availability.
- May incur additional charges.
- Must comply with supplier policies.

Certain promotional bookings cannot be modified.

---

## Pricing Policy

Displayed prices may include:

- Accommodation Charges
- Taxes
- Service Fees

Prices generally do not include:

- Visa Fees
- Travel Insurance
- Personal Expenses
- Airport Transfers (unless specified)
- Optional Activities

Prices remain subject to supplier changes until booking confirmation.

---

## Payment Requirement

Most bookings require:

- Full payment before confirmation

Certain holiday packages may allow:

- Partial advance payment
- Remaining balance before departure

Failure to complete payment within the specified deadline may result in automatic cancellation.

---

## Customer Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for:

- Providing accurate personal information.
- Carrying valid travel documents.
- Obtaining required visas.
- Arriving on time for flights and tours.
- Reviewing supplier-specific terms before travel.

---

## Booking Confirmation Documents

After confirmation, customers receive:

- Booking Reference Number
- Travel Itinerary
- Hotel Voucher
- Flight Ticket (if applicable)
- Tour Confirmation
- Payment Receipt

Customers should keep digital and printed copies during travel.

---

## Special Requests

TripPilot may forward requests such as:

- Early Check-in
- Late Check-out
- Airport Pickup
- Vegetarian Meals
- Wheelchair Assistance
- Baby Crib
- Anniversary or Birthday Arrangements

Special requests are subject to supplier availability and cannot be guaranteed.

---

## Important Notes

- Booking availability is not guaranteed until payment is completed.
- Prices may change before booking confirmation.
- Government-issued identification may be required during check-in.
- Some destinations charge mandatory local tourism taxes payable directly at the hotel.
- Customers should carefully review all booking details before confirming their reservation.
