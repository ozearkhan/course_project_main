# Travel Insurance Policy

## Overview

TripPilot strongly recommends purchasing travel insurance for all domestic and international trips. Travel insurance helps protect travelers against unexpected events such as medical emergencies, trip cancellations, baggage loss, travel delays, and other unforeseen circumstances.

Travel insurance is offered through trusted third-party insurance providers. The coverage, exclusions, and claim process are governed by the insurance provider's policy.

---

## Why Travel Insurance is Important

Travel insurance provides financial protection against unexpected situations that may occur before or during your journey.

Some common situations include:

- Medical emergencies
- Trip cancellations
- Flight delays
- Lost baggage
- Passport loss
- Personal accidents
- Emergency evacuation
- Travel interruptions

Without travel insurance, travelers may be responsible for paying these expenses out of pocket.

---

## Coverage Options

Travel insurance plans may include one or more of the following coverages.

### Medical Expenses

Coverage for:

- Hospitalization
- Emergency medical treatment
- Doctor consultations
- Prescription medications
- Emergency surgeries
- Ambulance services

Coverage limits depend on the selected insurance plan.

---

### Emergency Medical Evacuation

If medically necessary, the insurance provider may arrange transportation to the nearest suitable medical facility or the traveler's home country.

Coverage is subject to the insurer's terms and approval.

---

### Trip Cancellation

Coverage may reimburse eligible prepaid travel expenses if the trip is cancelled due to covered reasons such as:

- Serious illness
- Injury
- Death of an immediate family member
- Natural disasters
- Government travel restrictions
- Jury duty (where applicable)

Supporting documentation may be required.

---

### Trip Interruption

If a trip is interrupted after departure due to covered reasons, the insurance provider may reimburse unused travel expenses and additional transportation costs.

---

### Flight Delay

Coverage may reimburse reasonable expenses caused by significant flight delays, including:

- Meals
- Accommodation
- Local transportation

The delay duration required for eligibility varies by policy.

---

### Baggage Delay

If checked baggage is delayed beyond the period specified in the policy, reimbursement may be available for essential purchases such as:

- Clothing
- Toiletries
- Basic personal necessities

Receipts should be retained for reimbursement claims.

---

### Lost or Damaged Baggage

Coverage may compensate travelers for:

- Permanently lost baggage
- Stolen baggage
- Damaged baggage

Compensation limits depend on the policy and supporting documentation.

---

### Passport Loss

If a passport is lost or stolen during travel, insurance may assist with expenses related to:

- Emergency documentation
- Passport replacement
- Additional accommodation (if required)

Travelers should report the loss immediately to local authorities.

---

### Personal Liability

Certain policies may provide protection if the traveler becomes legally liable for accidental injury to another person or damage to third-party property during the trip.

Coverage varies by insurer.

---

## Common Exclusions

Travel insurance generally does not cover:

- Pre-existing medical conditions (unless specifically included)
- Self-inflicted injuries
- Illegal activities
- Alcohol or drug-related incidents
- Participation in unauthorized dangerous activities
- Travel against government travel advisories
- Cosmetic treatments
- Routine medical checkups
- Loss resulting from negligence

Customers should carefully review the complete policy wording before purchase.

---

## Claim Process

To submit a claim:

1. Notify the insurance provider as soon as possible.
2. Complete the claim form.
3. Submit all required documents.
4. Cooperate with any additional verification requested by the insurer.
5. Wait for claim assessment and approval.

---

## Required Documents for Claims

Depending on the claim type, documents may include:

- Insurance Policy Certificate
- Passport Copy
- Boarding Pass
- Flight Tickets
- Medical Reports
- Hospital Bills
- Police Report (for theft or loss)
- Property Irregularity Report (PIR) for baggage claims
- Receipts for eligible expenses
- Booking Confirmation

---

## Purchasing Travel Insurance

Travel insurance can usually be purchased:

- During TripPilot booking
- After booking but before departure
- Directly from the insurance provider

Coverage generally begins on the policy's effective date and ends on the policy expiration date.

---

## Customer Responsibilities

Customers should:

- Read the complete insurance policy before purchasing.
- Understand coverage limits and exclusions.
- Carry a digital or printed copy of the insurance certificate while traveling.
- Contact the insurance provider immediately during emergencies.
- Retain all receipts and supporting documents required for claims.

---

## Important Notes

- TripPilot facilitates the purchase of travel insurance but is not the insurance provider.
- All claims are evaluated solely by the insurance company according to the policy terms.
- Coverage limits, exclusions, and benefits vary depending on the selected insurance plan.
- Purchasing travel insurance does not guarantee approval of every claim.
- Travelers are encouraged to select a policy that adequately covers their destination, trip duration, and planned activities.
