# Tokyo Travel Guide

## Overview

Tokyo, the capital of Japan, is one of the world's most advanced and vibrant cities. It seamlessly blends centuries-old traditions with cutting-edge technology, offering visitors everything from historic temples and peaceful gardens to futuristic skyscrapers and bustling shopping districts. Tokyo is an excellent destination for families, solo travelers, business professionals, and food enthusiasts.

---

## Best Time to Visit

The best seasons to visit Tokyo are:

- March to May (Spring)
- October to November (Autumn)

Spring is famous for cherry blossom (Sakura) season, while autumn offers colorful foliage and pleasant weather.

Summer is warm and humid, whereas winter is generally cold but dry with occasional snowfall.

## Transportation

Tokyo has one of the world's most reliable public transportation systems.

Available transportation includes:

- JR Railway Lines
- Tokyo Metro
- Toei Subway
- Public Buses
- Airport Express Trains
- Licensed Taxis
- Ride-sharing services
- Bicycle Rentals

IC cards such as Suica and PASMO can be used across most transportation services.

---

## Currency

Currency: Japanese Yen

Currency Code: JPY

Although credit cards are accepted at most hotels, shopping malls, and restaurants, some small businesses still prefer cash payments.

ATMs are widely available throughout the city.

---

## Language

Official Language:

Japanese

English signage is available in most railway stations, airports, tourist attractions, and major hotels.

Translation applications can be helpful when visiting local markets and smaller restaurants.

---

## Safety Information

Tokyo is considered one of the safest cities in the world.

Travelers should:

- Follow public transport etiquette.
- Keep passports in a secure location.
- Carry cash for smaller establishments.
- Respect local customs and queue patiently.

Emergency Numbers:

Police: 110

Fire and Ambulance: 119

## Climate

Spring:
10°C – 22°C

Summer:
24°C – 35°C

Autumn:
12°C – 25°C

Winter:
2°C – 12°C

Light jackets are recommended during spring and autumn, while winter requires warmer clothing.

---

## Travel Tips

- Purchase a Suica or PASMO card for convenient travel.
- Avoid speaking loudly on trains.
- Dispose of garbage only in designated bins.
- Carry a portable Wi-Fi device or local SIM card for navigation.
- Visit popular attractions early to avoid crowds.

---

## Recommended Trip Duration

A stay of 5 to 7 days is recommended to explore Tokyo's major attractions, shopping districts, cultural sites, and nearby destinations such as Mount Fuji or Hakone.
