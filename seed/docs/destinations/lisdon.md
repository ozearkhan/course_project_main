# Lisbon Travel Guide

## Overview

Lisbon is the capital and largest city of Portugal, situated along the Atlantic coast. Known for its colorful neighborhoods, historic trams, scenic viewpoints, delicious seafood, and rich maritime history, Lisbon offers a perfect blend of traditional Portuguese culture and modern urban living. The city is an excellent destination for history enthusiasts, food lovers, photographers, digital nomads, and families looking for a relaxed European vacation.

---

## Best Time to Visit

The ideal months to visit Lisbon are:

- March to May (Spring)
- September to October (Autumn)

These seasons provide pleasant temperatures, fewer crowds, and ideal conditions for sightseeing and outdoor activities.

Summer (June to August) is the peak tourist season with warm temperatures and lively festivals, while winter remains mild compared to many European destinations.

## Transportation

Lisbon has a well-developed public transportation network.

Available transportation includes:

- Lisbon Metro
- Historic Trams
- Public Buses
- Funicular Railways
- Ferry Services
- Commuter Trains
- Licensed Taxis
- Ride-sharing Services

The Viva Viagem Card offers convenient access to most public transportation services throughout the city.

## Safety Information

Lisbon is generally considered a safe city for tourists.

Visitors should:

- Stay alert in crowded public transport areas.
- Keep personal belongings secure in tourist locations.
- Use licensed taxis or trusted ride-sharing applications.
- Carry copies of important travel documents.

Emergency Number:

112

## Travel Tips

- Purchase a Viva Viagem Card for economical public transportation.
- Ride the historic Tram 28 early in the morning to avoid long queues.
- Wear comfortable footwear, as many streets are steep and paved with cobblestones.
- Try authentic Portuguese cuisine at local restaurants instead of tourist-focused establishments.
- Visit nearby destinations such as Sintra or Cascais for memorable day trips.
- Carry a light jacket during evenings, even in spring and autumn.

---

## Recommended Trip Duration

A stay of 3 to 5 days is recommended to explore Lisbon's historical landmarks, museums, waterfront areas, shopping districts, cultural neighborhoods, and nearby attractions such as Sintra and Cascais.
