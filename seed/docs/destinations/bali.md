# Bali Travel Guide

## Overview

Bali is one of Indonesia's most popular tourist destinations, known for its tropical beaches, lush rice terraces, volcanic mountains, ancient temples, and vibrant cultural heritage. The island offers a perfect combination of relaxation, adventure, luxury resorts, wellness retreats, and outdoor activities. Whether you're looking for surfing, scuba diving, cultural experiences, or a peaceful getaway, Bali caters to every type of traveler.

---

## Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit Bali is during the dry season:

- April to October

These months offer sunny weather, lower humidity, and ideal conditions for beach activities and sightseeing.

The rainy season runs from November to March, bringing frequent tropical showers but fewer tourists.

## Transportation

Transportation options in Bali include:

- Private Taxi Services
- Ride-sharing Apps
- Scooter Rentals
- Car Rentals
- Hotel Shuttle Services
- Private Tour Operators

Public transportation is limited, so many visitors prefer taxis, rental scooters, or private drivers for daily travel.

---

## Currency

Currency: Indonesian Rupiah

Currency Code: IDR

Credit cards are accepted at hotels, shopping malls, and major restaurants. Smaller shops, local markets, and street vendors often accept cash only.

ATMs are available in most tourist areas.

---

## Language

Official Language:

Indonesian (Bahasa Indonesia)

English is widely spoken in hotels, restaurants, resorts, shopping centers, and tourist attractions.

---

- Mie Goreng

- Satay
- Nasi Campur

## Shopping

Popular shopping destinations include:

- Ubud Art Market
- Seminyak Village
- Beachwalk Shopping Center
- Sukawati Art Market
- Kuta Shopping District

Visitors commonly purchase handcrafted souvenirs, wooden sculptures, batik clothing, silver jewelry, coffee, spices, and local artwork.

---

## Safety Information

Bali is generally a safe destination for tourists.

Visitors should:

- Drink bottled water.
- Wear sunscreen and stay hydrated.
- Respect local customs when visiting temples.
- Secure valuables in crowded tourist areas.
- Wear helmets while riding scooters.

Emergency Numbers:

Police: 110

Ambulance: 118

Fire: 113

---

## Climate

Dry Season:
26°C – 32°C

Rainy Season:
24°C – 31°C

The climate is tropical throughout the year with consistently warm temperatures.

---

## Travel Tips

- Wear modest clothing when entering temples.
- Remove footwear before entering religious sites where required.
- Carry insect repellent for outdoor activities.
- Book water sports and island tours in advance during peak season.
- Exchange currency only at authorized exchange centers.
- Keep digital and physical copies of your passport.

---

## Recommended Trip Duration

A stay of 5 to 7 days is recommended to explore Bali's beaches, temples, waterfalls, cultural villages, adventure activities, and nearby islands such as Nusa Penida and the Gili Islands.
