# Airport FAQ

## Overview

This document answers frequently asked questions about airport procedures, check-in, security screening, boarding, baggage, and arrival processes. Airport procedures may vary depending on the airline, airport, and destination, so travelers should always follow the instructions provided by airport authorities and airline staff.

---

## 1. How early should I arrive at the airport?

Recommended arrival times are:

| Flight Type | Recommended Arrival Time |
|--------------|--------------------------|
| Domestic Flight | 2 hours before departure |
| International Flight | 3 hours before departure |

During peak travel seasons or holidays, arriving earlier is advisable.

---

## 2. What documents should I carry to the airport?

For international travel, you should carry:

- Valid Passport
- Visa (if required)
- Flight Ticket or E-ticket
- Boarding Pass (if checked in online)
- Travel Insurance Documents (if applicable)
- Government-issued Identification
- Hotel Booking Confirmation (recommended)

Keep both digital and printed copies whenever possible.

---

## 3. What happens during check-in?

At check-in, the airline will:

- Verify your travel documents.
- Confirm your booking.
- Issue your boarding pass (if not already issued).
- Check and tag your baggage.
- Assign your seat (if applicable).

Online check-in is available with many airlines and can reduce waiting time at the airport.

---

## 4. What is a boarding pass?

A boarding pass is a document issued by the airline that allows you to board your flight.

It typically includes:

- Passenger Name
- Flight Number
- Departure Gate
- Boarding Time
- Seat Number
- Boarding Group (if applicable)

Always verify the details before proceeding to security.

---

## 5. What happens during airport security screening?

All passengers and carry-on baggage must pass through security screening.

You may be required to:

- Place electronic devices in a separate tray.
- Remove jackets or coats.
- Remove belts or metal accessories if requested.
- Present liquids for inspection.
- Walk through a metal detector or body scanner.

Follow instructions from airport security personnel at all times.

---

## 6. What items are restricted in carry-on baggage?

Common restricted items include:

- Sharp objects
- Firearms
- Explosives
- Flammable materials
- Large liquid containers
- Certain tools
- Hazardous chemicals

Security rules may vary by country and airport.

---

## 7. What should I do if I miss my flight?

If you miss your flight:

1. Contact the airline immediately.
2. Visit the airline's service counter.
3. Ask about rebooking options.
4. Review the fare conditions of your ticket.
5. Contact your travel insurance provider if applicable.

Additional charges may apply depending on the airline's policy.

---

## 8. What is the boarding process?

Once boarding begins:

- Listen for boarding announcements.
- Proceed to the boarding gate.
- Present your boarding pass and identification.
- Follow airline staff instructions.
- Board according to your assigned boarding group if applicable.

Passengers arriving after the boarding gate closes may not be permitted to board.

---

## 9. What happens after landing?

After arrival, international travelers typically proceed through:

1. Immigration
2. Passport Control
3. Baggage Claim
4. Customs Inspection
5. Arrival Hall

Follow airport signage and instructions from airport staff.

---

## 10. What should I do if my baggage is delayed or lost?

If your baggage does not arrive:

1. Visit the airline's baggage service desk.
2. Report the missing baggage immediately.
3. Complete a Property Irregularity Report (PIR).
4. Retain baggage claim tags.
5. Keep contact details for future updates.

The airline will assist in tracking your baggage according to its policy.

---

## 11. Can I carry food through airport security?

Packaged food is generally permitted, but restrictions may apply depending on:

- Destination country
- Customs regulations
- Security policies

Liquids and semi-liquid foods are subject to liquid restrictions.

---

## 12. Can I use electronic devices during my flight?

Electronic device usage depends on airline policies.

Generally:

- Mobile phones must be switched to Airplane Mode.
- Larger electronic devices may need to be stored during takeoff and landing.
- In-flight Wi-Fi may be available on some flights.

Always follow instructions from the cabin crew.

---

## 13. What should I do if my flight is delayed?

If your flight is delayed:

- Monitor airport information displays.
- Check the airline's mobile app or website.
- Stay near the departure gate.
- Contact the airline for updated departure information.
- Retain receipts for eligible expenses if covered by your travel insurance.

---

## Travel Tips

- Keep your passport and boarding pass easily accessible.
- Charge your mobile devices before arriving at the airport.
- Label all checked baggage with your contact information.
- Follow airport announcements carefully.
- Keep valuables, medications, and important documents in your carry-on baggage.
- Check your departure gate periodically, as gate assignments may change.
