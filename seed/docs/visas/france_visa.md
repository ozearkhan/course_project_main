# France Tourist Visa

## Overview

A France Tourist Visa, also known as a Schengen Short-Stay Visa (Type C), allows eligible foreign nationals to visit France and other Schengen countries for tourism, leisure, family visits, or short business trips. The visa generally permits stays of up to 90 days within any 180-day period.

Indian passport holders are required to obtain a valid Schengen visa before traveling to France.

---

## Visa Type

Visa Category:
Short-Stay Schengen Visa (Type C)

Purpose of Travel:

- Tourism
- Family Visit
- Sightseeing
- Cultural Events
- Short Business Meetings

Maximum Stay:

90 days within a 180-day period.

---

## Eligibility

Applicants should:

- Hold a valid passport.
- Demonstrate sufficient financial resources.
- Have a genuine purpose for visiting France.
- Intend to leave the Schengen Area before the visa expires.
- Possess valid travel medical insurance.
- Meet all immigration requirements established by French authorities.

Visa approval is solely at the discretion of the French Embassy or Consulate.

---

## Required Documents

Applicants should prepare the following documents:

- Valid Passport
- Completed Visa Application Form
- Recent Passport-sized Photographs
- Confirmed Flight Reservation
- Hotel Booking Confirmation or Invitation Letter
- Detailed Travel Itinerary
- Travel Medical Insurance
- Last Six Months Bank Statements
- Salary Slips or Proof of Income
- Income Tax Returns (if applicable)
- Employment Letter or Student Identification
- Cover Letter explaining the purpose of travel

Additional documents may be requested during the application process.

---

## Passport Requirements

The passport must:

- Be valid for at least three months after the intended departure from the Schengen Area.
- Contain at least two blank visa pages.
- Be issued within the last ten years.
- Be in good physical condition.

---

## Processing Time

Typical processing time:

15 to 21 calendar days.

Applications submitted during peak travel seasons may require additional processing time.

Applicants are encouraged to apply at least four weeks before their intended travel date.

---

## Visa Fees

Applicants are required to pay:

- Standard Schengen Visa Fee
- Applicable Visa Application Centre (VAC) Service Charges

Visa fees are generally non-refundable, even if the application is refused.

---

## Travel Insurance Requirements

Applicants must provide travel medical insurance that:

- Covers all Schengen countries.
- Includes emergency medical treatment.
- Covers hospitalization.
- Includes emergency medical evacuation.
- Meets the minimum coverage required by Schengen regulations.

Insurance must remain valid throughout the entire duration of the trip.

---

## Financial Requirements

Applicants should demonstrate sufficient financial resources to cover:

- Accommodation
- Meals
- Local Transportation
- Sightseeing
- Emergency Expenses

Bank statements should reflect stable financial activity over the previous six months.

---

## Important Notes

- A visa does not guarantee entry into France.
- Immigration officers may request supporting documents upon arrival.
- Travelers should carry copies of hotel bookings, return flight tickets, travel insurance, and proof of sufficient funds.
- Overstaying a Schengen visa may result in fines, deportation, or future visa restrictions.
- Travelers must comply with all immigration and customs regulations during their stay.

---

## Visa Application Tips

- Submit accurate and complete documentation.
- Ensure all bookings and personal information are consistent across documents.
- Purchase travel insurance before submitting the application.
- Apply well in advance of your planned departure date.
- Keep digital and printed copies of all submitted documents for future reference.
