# Indonesia Tourist Visa

## Overview

An Indonesia Tourist Visa allows eligible foreign nationals to visit Indonesia for tourism, leisure, sightseeing, family visits, and short recreational trips. Depending on the traveler's nationality, entry may be permitted through a Visa on Arrival (VoA), an Electronic Visa (e-Visa), or a Tourist Visit Visa obtained before travel.

Indian passport holders are generally eligible to apply for an appropriate tourist visa before departure or obtain a Visa on Arrival at designated Indonesian international airports and seaports, subject to the latest immigration regulations.

---

## Visa Type

Visa Category:

Tourist Visit Visa

Purpose of Travel:

- Tourism
- Leisure
- Visiting Friends or Family
- Cultural Activities
- Sightseeing

Maximum Stay:

- Initial Stay: Up to 30 days
- Extension: One additional 30-day extension may be available, subject to Indonesian immigration approval.

Travelers should verify the latest entry requirements before planning their trip.

---

## Eligibility

Applicants should:

- Hold a valid passport.
- Have a genuine purpose for visiting Indonesia.
- Possess sufficient financial resources.
- Hold a confirmed return or onward ticket.
- Comply with Indonesian immigration laws.
- Intend to leave Indonesia before the authorized stay expires.

Final entry approval rests with Indonesian immigration authorities.

---

## Required Documents

Applicants should prepare:

- Valid Passport
- Completed Visa Application (if applicable)
- Recent Passport-sized Photograph
- Confirmed Return Flight Ticket
- Hotel Reservation or Accommodation Details
- Travel Itinerary
- Bank Statements for the Last Three to Six Months
- Proof of Employment or Student Identification (if applicable)
- Cover Letter (if requested)

Immigration authorities may request additional documentation depending on individual circumstances.

---

## Passport Requirements

The passport must:

- Be valid for at least six months from the date of entry.
- Contain at least two blank pages.
- Be in good physical condition without damage.

Travelers should carry both printed and digital copies of important travel documents.

---

## Processing Time

Typical processing time:

- e-Visa: 3 to 7 working days
- Tourist Visa: 5 to 10 working days

Processing times may vary during holiday periods or due to additional verification requirements.

Applicants are encouraged to apply at least three weeks before departure.

---

## Visa Fees

Applicants are required to pay:

- Applicable Tourist Visa Fee
- Visa on Arrival Fee (if applicable)
- Processing Charges

Visa fees are generally non-refundable once the application has been submitted.

---

## Financial Requirements

Applicants should demonstrate sufficient funds to cover:

- Accommodation
- Meals
- Local Transportation
- Tourism Activities
- Emergency Expenses

Bank statements should reflect stable financial activity and the ability to support the planned trip.

---

## Health and Travel Insurance

Travel insurance is strongly recommended and should ideally include:

- Emergency Medical Treatment
- Hospitalization
- Medical Evacuation
- Trip Cancellation
- Lost or Delayed Baggage
- Personal Liability Coverage

Travelers should also review any health advisories or vaccination recommendations before departure.

---

## Entry Requirements

Upon arrival, immigration officers may request:

- Passport
- Valid Visa or Visa on Arrival Approval
- Return or Onward Flight Ticket
- Accommodation Details
- Proof of Financial Resources

Travelers should ensure all supporting documents are readily available during immigration inspection.

---

## Important Notes

- A tourist visa does not permit employment or business activities.
- Visitors must comply with Indonesian immigration regulations throughout their stay.
- Overstaying the permitted duration may result in fines, detention, deportation, or future travel restrictions.
- Respect local customs, traditions, and religious practices while visiting different regions of Indonesia.
- Keep your passport and immigration documents secure throughout your trip.

---

## Visa Application Tips

- Apply well before your intended travel date.
- Ensure your passport has sufficient validity.
- Keep printed copies of hotel reservations and return flight tickets.
- Purchase comprehensive travel insurance before departure.
- Monitor official Indonesian immigration announcements for any changes to visa requirements or entry regulations.
